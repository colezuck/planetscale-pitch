# Cold call — Honeycomb / Fred Hebert

Working source: [Cold Call Prep in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/3f01840cbb9e80dba58bd1adcae19148). Reconciled October 5, 2026.

## Script

**Intro**

“Hey Fred, this is Zuckowsky calling from PlanetScale. How are you?”

**What we do**

“Good. We work with engineering teams at companies like Cash App and Cursor. We manage and run their databases so their engineers can spend more time building product features and less time on database maintenance and scaling.

Is that something you’d be remotely interested in looking at?”

**If yes → book the meeting**

“Great. Would you have 20 minutes Wednesday or Thursday to see whether we could take some of that database work off your team’s plate?”

**If someone else owns it**

“Who would be the right person? Would you be comfortable with me reaching out and saying you pointed me their way?”

## Honeycomb research

- **Product:** observability for debugging production systems using logs, metrics, and traces. [Platform overview](https://www.honeycomb.io/platform)
- **AI:** Canvas supports AI-assisted investigations; MCP connects telemetry to coding assistants. [Honeycomb Intelligence](https://www.honeycomb.io/platform)
- **Architecture:** Honeycomb describes a distributed column store for telemetry. We haven’t established its application database stack. [Architecture](https://www.honeycomb.io/blog/why-observability-requires-distributed-column-store)

Customer proof: [Cash App](https://planetscale.com/case-studies/cash-app) and [Cursor](https://planetscale.com/).
