# Cold-call preparation

Start with [requirements](../brief/README.md) and [Honeycomb account context](../../accounts/honeycomb/README.md).

This folder will hold the opener, reason for calling, objection branches, meeting/referral ask, and rehearsal feedback. Scripts have not been authored yet.

The scenario calls Fred Hebert. Earn a specific 20-minute conversation or a referral with permission to use his name. Prepare for two three-minute attempts with feedback between them. Keep account facts in the shared account folder; record what changed after feedback here.
