## Speaker notes 3

The goal here isn't to see if you memorized Metal. It's to see if you can take something technical, understand it well enough to explain it in your own words, and hold up when an engineer pushes back. That's most of the job.
A good five minutes sounds like a conversation, not a pitch. You tell the engineer what Metal is in a sentence he'd repeat to a coworker. You pick one problem he probably has — slow queries under load, a surprise storage or IOPS bill, a database that gets unpredictable when traffic spikes — and connect Metal to that problem. You say what it's not for before he asks. And when he asks something you don't know, you say so and move on. One clean "I don't know" beats a confident wrong answer every time.
What we don't want: reading the website back to us, listing features, or talking for five minutes without checking whether any of it landed.
