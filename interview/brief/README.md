# Final-round interview requirements

Source: [Original PowerPoint](SDR-Final-Round-Candidate.pptx), including speaker notes. See [complete extracted text](extracted-text.md). Imported October 4, 2026.

The overall session is 60 minutes with Will Cronin and Kevan Zadeh. The listed exercise blocks total 50 minutes; the brief reserves time for questions and feedback.

| Exercise | Timing | Success criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metal teach-back | 15-minute block; five-minute explanation and five-minute questions specified | Explain Metal in your own words to a staff engineer running Postgres on Aurora. Connect one problem to the mechanism, volunteer tradeoffs, check understanding, acknowledge uncertainty honestly. |
| Cold call | 10-minute block; 3-minute call, 2-minute feedback, 3-minute repeat specified | Call Fred Hebert at Honeycomb. Earn a 20-minute conversation for a specific reason or a referral with permission to use his name. Incorporate feedback on the repeat. |
| Discovery | 25 minutes | Meet Steph Hippo after Fred's referral. Establish a decision process, a problem worth solving, a reason to act now, and a next step involving the right people. Notes specify why anything, why now, and why PlanetScale. |
